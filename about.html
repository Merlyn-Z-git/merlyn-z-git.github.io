About Me
关于作者

Merlyn

你好，我是 Merlyn，一名独立创作者，主要创作漫画与小说。

我对以人物为中心的叙事很感兴趣，尤其关注人物关系、个体意识，以及人们如何理解自我与周围的世界。

我的创作形式包括漫画、小说和实验性叙事作品。

I'm Merlyn, an independent creator working primarily with comics and fiction.

I'm interested in character-driven storytelling, particularly the complexities of human relationships, individual consciousness, and the ways people understand themselves and the world around them.

My work takes various forms, including comics, fiction, and experimental narratives.

About This Archive
关于本站

Merlyn Archive 是一个用于保存和整理个人创作的档案空间。在这里，我会收录自己的漫画、小说、角色设定、故事记录，以及其他尚未归类的创作片段。

它不局限于某一种媒介或创作形式，而是希望能够持续记录不同作品从构思到发展的过程。

有些作品已经完成，有些仍在进行中，也有一些或许永远不会完成。它们都是这个档案的一部分。

Merlyn Archive is a personal archive dedicated to my creative work. It serves as a place to collect and organize my comics, stories, character profiles, and other fragments of ideas.

Rather than focusing on a single medium or format, this archive is intended to preserve different forms of expression as they develop over time.

Some works are complete, some are still in progress, and others may remain unfinished. All of them are part of this archive.
